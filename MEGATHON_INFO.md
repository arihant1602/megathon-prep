# Megathon X: Singularity (2026)

Source: https://megathon.in/ (fetched 2026-10-09)

## Event
- **Dates:** 10–11 Oct 2026, 24 hours, on-site only at IIIT Hyderabad
- **Organizer:** E-Cell, IIIT Hyderabad (running since 2016)
- **Prize pool:** ₹8 Lakhs+ (2025: ₹7L, 2024: ₹4L, 2023: ₹4L)
- **Team:** 1–5 students, cross-college teams allowed. Teams are not allotted.
- **Eligibility:** every member must be a currently enrolled student in India
- **Submission:** working prototype plus in-person pitch. The core flow must run.
- **Rules:** any language or framework. AI tools are allowed but **must be disclosed** in the code and the presentation.
- **Problem statements are revealed at the venue** and are not published in advance.
- Registration: https://konfhub.com/megathon-26

## Past Problem Statements

### 2025 (Deccan Edition, 11–12 Oct): Title CHUBB, plus Qualcomm, Bhashini, Saral AI (social)
About 1,100 students from 62 colleges. The top 20 teams pitched.
1. **Bhashini:** a mobile or responsive app using Bhashini ASR/MT/TTS/OCR, covering 4 or more scenarios in one domain (tourism, healthcare, agri, or banking)
2. **Qualcomm:** an Android app that restyles camera snapshots from a text prompt, with all models **on-device NPU** (Qualcomm Innovators Dev Kit) and no cloud APIs
3. **Chubb (Claims):** multimodal vehicle-damage assessment (detection, severity, cost band) plus text claims plus tampered-photo detection, with Grad-CAM or saliency explainability, public datasets only
4. **Chubb (Churn):** auto-insurance churn probability with SHAP/LIME reason codes and a non-technical dashboard
5. **SARAL:** research paper to video presentation, using the open-source Democratise Research pipeline (slides, narration, video)
6. **OSDG:** a collaborative rich text editor **in C**, with live cursors, read/write auth, conflict handling, and offline edits that merge on reconnect

### 2023 (Nov): Qualcomm, KonnectNXT, Telangana Govt, CIE
About 400 students from 30 colleges. The top 6 per PS (18 teams) made the finals.
- LLM-based medical query system on **edge devices** (likely Qualcomm)
- Candidate profile analysis with ML plus psychometric tests (likely KonnectNXT)
- Telangana Govt (social): tracking paddy cultivation from satellite imagery
- Winners: Pandavas (KonnectNXT), Pikachu (Qualcomm), Ligmoid (Telangana)

### 2022: Title Stellantis, Associate Qualcomm, social RCTS
- 2 Stellantis PS, 1 Qualcomm PS, 1 social PS (Raj Reddy Centre for Tech & Society)
- Winners: AlphaQueue and Pied Piper (Stellantis), Le Boys (Qualcomm), Hack Your Mom and What the Hack (social)

### 2017
- 5 PS: smart cities, privacy and security, women's safety, and others

## Patterns / Prep Guesses (speculation)
- **Qualcomm appears every year** with an on-device or edge AI track (NPU, no cloud). Prep options: QNN/QAI Hub, ONNX/TFLite quantized models on Android, running small LLMs or diffusion models on device.
- **Bhashini returns.** Get API access now and test the ASR/MT/TTS/OCR pipelines in advance (https://bhashini.gov.in/).
- **Lloyds and Vanguard are finance companies.** Expect banking or investing problems like fraud detection, risk, personal finance assistants, or explainable ML with SHAP/LIME (similar to Chubb's 2025 tracks).
- Explainability and a non-technical dashboard were explicit requirements in 2025, so keep a dashboard template ready (Streamlit or Next.js).
- There is usually one systems or social track (OSDG in C, gov satellite data).
